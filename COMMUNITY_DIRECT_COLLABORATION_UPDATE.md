# Community Direct Collaboration

Accepted community members with a non-viewer membership now receive direct roadmap collaboration access.

- Public visitors remain view-only.
- A community join request still requires owner approval.
- After approval, the member receives a contributor roadmap share and can open the editor directly.
- No second roadmap collaboration request is required.
- Backend access and mutation checks treat approved community membership as direct collaboration.
- Existing teams retain their direct-collaboration behavior.
